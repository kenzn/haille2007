About Us

A scholarship hub built by former applicants.

DONZA HUB started in 2025 when one university student (Haille Kensany) got tired of watching capable students give up on funding because the process was confusing. We rebuilt it from scratch: one form, transparent criteria, and a mentor on every file.

◎

Our mission

Remove the paperwork barrier between motivated students and the funding they've earned, and pair every scholar with a mentor for the year they're funded.

✓

How review works

Every application is read by two independent reviewers from our mentor network — never an algorithm alone — and scored against the same public rubric.

01

Transparency

Our scoring rubric is public. You'll always know what you're being measured against.

02

Access first

No application fee, no minimum GPA cutoff, no institution restriction.

03

Mentorship

Every award comes with a mentor check-in, not just a one-time cheque.

04

Speed

Rolling decisions — most applicants hear back inside three weeks.

Accepted formats: PDF, JPG or PNG, 10MB max per file. You can come back and finish this later using the link from step 1.

    Is there an application fee?

    No. DONZA HUB never charges to apply, review, or disburse a scholarship. Anyone asking you to pay is not us.

    Can I apply if I'm still waiting on admission results?

    Yes — start your application now and upload proof of admission once you receive it, before the review deadline.

    How is the award amount decided?

    Reviewers score demonstrated need and initiative against our public rubric; award size follows the score band, not a fixed formula.

    What if I miss a document?

    Your file stays in draft. You'll get a reminder email and can finish uploading anytime before the deadline.
